# Implementation Task Breakdown

## 1) Goal

Break the MVP into manageable workstreams so the team can implement the system in incremental milestones without creating unmanageable dependencies.

## 2) Workstream 1 — Foundation
- User authentication and roles
- Branch and location setup
- Basic settings and configuration
- Audit log infrastructure
- Backup and restore foundation

## 3) Workstream 2 — Product and Catalog
- Product creation and editing
- Category management
- Barcode and internal code handling
- Price and cost records
- Product search and filtering

## 4) Workstream 3 — Supplier and Purchase
- Supplier creation
- Purchase invoice and items
- Currency and exchange rate handling
- Batch creation on receipt
- Purchase payment status tracking

## 5) Workstream 4 — Inventory and Batch Control
- Stock ledger
- FEFO logic
- Expiry warning rules
- Stock availability checks
- Reorder threshold monitoring

## 6) Workstream 5 — POS and Sales
- Shift open/close flow
- POS invoice creation
- Product selection and quantity entry
- Payment capture
- Receipt generation
- Invoice closure

## 7) Workstream 6 — Returns and Reconciliation
- Return transaction creation
- Stock reversal
- Cash impact update
- Reconciliation reporting

## 8) Workstream 7 — Reporting and Dashboard
- Daily sales report
- Inventory summary
- Expiry risk list
- Shift cash report
- Audit queries

## 9) Workstream 8 — Documents and Patient Lite
- Document upload
- Prescription archive
- Patient basic registration
- Linking document to invoice or patient

## 10) Task Sequencing Recommendation

### Sprint 1
- Foundation
- Catalog
- Supplier setup

### Sprint 2
- Inventory and batches
- Purchase flow

### Sprint 3
- POS sales and payments
- Shift closure and reconciliation

### Sprint 4
- Returns and stock reversal
- Reporting and audit

### Sprint 5
- Document upload and patient lite
- UAT and stabilisation

## 11) Exit Criteria for MVP

The MVP is ready when all core workstreams are integrated, pass scenario testing, and support complete daily pharmacy workflow without clinic complexity.
