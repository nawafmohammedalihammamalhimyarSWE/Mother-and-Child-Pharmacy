# Implementation Roadmap

## 1) Objective

Translate the approved scope and business requirements into a realistic delivery plan, with an emphasis on pharmacy MVP readiness and controlled future expansion into clinic modules.

## 2) Phase Breakdown

### Phase 0 — Discovery and Vision
- Project framing
- Stakeholder review
- Vision and non-goals
- Initial business and technical assumptions
- Decision and risk registers

### Phase 1 — Operations and Gap Analysis
- Process analysis
- User role review
- Gap assessment
- Decision checklist
- Business process validation

### Phase 2 — BRD and SRS
- Business requirements document
- Software requirements specification
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- Requirements review and sign-off

### Phase 3 — UX and Design
- User stories and journeys
- Screen flow design
- Wireframes and validation
- Arabic RTL design rules

### Phase 4 — Architecture and Security
- Local-first technical architecture
- Security controls
- Audit model
- Backup and recovery strategy

### Phase 5 — Development Planning
- Module decomposition
- Sprint breakdown
- Task ownership
- Dependency mapping

### Phase 6 — MVP Build
- Catalog and product setup
- Supplier and purchase flow
- Inventory and batch tracking
- POS and sales workflow
- Returns and cash/shift handling
- Reporting and audit logs

### Phase 7 — Testing and UAT
- Functional validation
- Regression and scenario tests
- Business acceptance testing
- Fixes and sign-off

### Phase 8 — Go-Live
- Production setup
- Data migration if needed
- Training and support
- Go-live monitoring

## 3) MVP Scope Priorities

### Priority 1
- Product catalog
- Batch and expiry management
- Purchase and suppliers
- Inventory ledger
- POS sales
- Returns and cash
- Reports and audit

### Priority 2
- HTML/PDF/Excel import review
- Paper prescription archive
- Patient Lite registration

### Priority 3
- Clinic scheduling and doctor contracts
- Electronic prescriptions
- Advanced medical modules

## 4) Delivery Recommendations

- Deliver in vertical slices, not as one giant release
- Build product catalog and stock controls first
- Validate sales and audit before expanding to advanced features
- Avoid adding clinic features to the MVP scope
- Maintain strict review gates before import or stock changes

## 5) Exit Criteria for MVP

The MVP is ready when:
- Product catalog is complete and searchable
- Batch and expiry rules are enforced
- Supplier and purchase records are working
- POS, payment, and returns are usable
- Shift and cash reconciliation are functional
- Audit trails and reports are available
- System works offline-first

## 6) Conclusion

This roadmap ensures that the pharmacy core is delivered safely and built for future growth without breaking business focus. The clinic should remain a future roadmap item until the pharmacy foundation is stable, validated, and approved.
